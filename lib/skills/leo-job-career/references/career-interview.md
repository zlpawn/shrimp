# Structured Career Interview Guide

## Principles
1. One question at a time. Never overwhelm with a multi-part questionnaire.
2. Anchor in concrete projects and events rather than abstract self-evaluations.
3. Focus questions on areas that provide high-value evidence for Java + Agent and P7 defense:
   - Complex business domain modeling
   - Cross-system transaction consistency and data migration
   - High availability, failure isolation, and recovery
   - Team technical leadership and architectural decisions
4. Separate what candidate personally implemented from what the team delivered.
5. Record metrics only when confirmed by the candidate; never fabricate numbers.
