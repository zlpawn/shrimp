# Job Quality Policy

## Hard Exclusions
- Explicit outsourcing keywords: 驻场开发, 派驻客户, 客户现场办公, 人力外包, 劳务派遣, 软件外包, 介意外包勿投, 现场驻场, 项目驻点, 项目制外派.
- Industry / Company keywords: 人力资源服务, 劳务派遣, 人才派遣, 外包服务.
- Listed / Mega Outsourcing Blacklist: 中软国际, 软通动力, 法本信息, 诚迈科技, 博彦科技, 拓保软件, 文思海辉, 润和软件, 易宝软件, 宇信科技, 中科创达, 上海思芮, 北京先进数通, 亚信科技, 信必优, 柯莱特, 千方科技等.
- Language mismatch: Python-only algorithm roles with zero Java stack relevance.

## Relevance Grading
- Java: Primary, Accepted, Adjacent, Minor, Python_only.
- Agent: Core, Supporting, Keyword_only, None.
- Freshness: Grade A (Active today/now), B (Active within 3 days/week), C (Within 2 weeks), D (Within month), E (Stale/inactive).
