# Progressive Preparation Planning

## Effort Curve
- Weekends 1-2: 4-5 hours/week (Market recon, project inventory).
- Weekends 3-4: 6-8 hours/week (Agent project core: RAG, MCP, workflows).
- Weekends 5-8: 8-10 hours/week (Evaluation, observability, JVM/distributed systems deep dive, P7 story defense).
- Formal interviews and phone screens are tracked separately.

## One Deliverable per Weekend
Every weekend must end with an independently verifiable artifact (a working module, an architecture doc, a refined story, a benchmark report).
