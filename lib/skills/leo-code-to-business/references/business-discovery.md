# Business Discovery

## Purpose

Turn verified repository facts into business capabilities, use cases, rules, lifecycle meaning, and
use-case families.

## Mandatory Outputs

For each use case establish who acts, why, what starts it, how decisions change behavior, what data
and states change, what external effects occur, what success/failure means, and which variants or
repair paths exist.

Group by shared business goal and lifecycle, not package layout. Search alternate channels,
background jobs, callbacks, operations, unbind/rebind, retries, and compensation before declaring a
family complete.

## Gate

Technical narration without actor, goal, decisions, outcome, and evidence is invalid.
